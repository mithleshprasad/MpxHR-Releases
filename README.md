## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Laptop.png" width="28" style="vertical-align: middle;" /> What is MpxHR Enterprise?

**MpxHR Enterprise** is complete, lightning-fast, and secure desktop software built specifically for Indian MSMEs, manufacturing units, CA firms, logistics companies, and corporate offices.

Unlike cloud payroll tools (greytHR, Zoho People, Keka) that charge recurring monthly per-employee rental fees and upload sensitive salary data to remote third-party servers, **MpxHR runs 100% offline directly on your Windows PC**. Your sensitive employee records, biometric logs, and bank account details **never leave your office computer**.

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Money%20Bag.png" width="28" style="vertical-align: middle;" /> Core Capabilities & Features

### 1. 🇮🇳 Indian Statutory Payroll Engine
- **Automated Salary Computations:** Basic, HRA, DA, Gross Salary, and Net Pay calculation in 1-click.
- **EPFO & ESIC Compliance:** Automatic calculation of Employee & Employer PF contributions and ESIC deductions.
- **EPFO ECR File Generator:** Direct 1-click export of official EPFO Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) text format ready to upload to the EPFO portal.
- **TDS & State-wise PT:** Built-in Professional Tax slabs for all Indian states and TDS computation.
- **Designer Payslips:** Export professional PDF and editable Microsoft Word (`.docx`) payslips with company logo and signature stamps.

### 2. 📍 Biometric Attendance & Shift Management
- **Biometric Device Integration:** Easily import logs from biometric fingerprint, face, and RFID attendance machines via USB or Excel/CSV.
- **Shift & Roster Automation:** Multi-shift planning, rotational shifts, grace periods, half-day rules, and automatic late mark deductions.
- **Leave Ledger:** Auto-track Paid Leave (PL), Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), Comp-off, and Loss-of-Pay (LOP) seamlessly synced with payroll.

### 3. 🔒 100% Data Privacy & Security
- **Local Storage:** Encrypted SQLite/local database file stored securely on your own hard drive.
- **Automatic Backups:** Daily backup created every time MpxHR closes, with optional 1-click sync to your private Google Drive.
- **Zero Lock-In Guarantee:** If your subscription ends, MpxHR enters a permanent **free view-only mode**. You can always view, print, and export all historical payroll records to Excel.

### 4. 📊 Performance Appraisals & SOP Library
- **KPI Scorecards:** Weighted Key Performance Indicator (KPI) evaluations with self & manager reviews.
- **SOP Management:** Built-in Standard Operating Procedure library with version controls and employee digital sign-off.
