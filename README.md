## 🚀 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Indian Statutory Payroll Engine (`indian-payroll-software`, `pf-esi-calculator`)
- **Automated Calculations:** Gross salary, Net pay, Employee & Employer PF contributions, ESIC, Professional Tax (state-wise PT slabs), and TDS deductions.
- **PF ECR File Generation:** Direct 1-click export of the official EPFO Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) text format ready for upload on the EPFO employer portal.
- **Form 16 & Tax Computation:** Integrated annual tax summary, deductions under 80C, 80D, standard deduction, and Form 16 generator.
- **Payslip Designer:** Generate professional PDF and editable Microsoft Word (`.docx`) payslips with company logo and signature stamp.

### 2. Biometric Attendance & Leave Management (`biometric-attendance`, `offline-payroll`)
- **Biometric Device Integration:** Import logs from biometric fingerprint and face attendance machines via USB or Excel/CSV.
- **Shift & Roster Management:** Multi-shift planning, rotational shifts, grace periods, half-day rules, and automatic late mark deductions.
- **Leave Ledger:** Paid leave (PL), casual leave (CL), sick leave (SL), compensatory off, and loss-of-pay (LOP) auto-linked with salary processing.

### 3. Performance Appraisals & SOP Library (`windows-hrms`)
- **KPI Scorecards:** Weighted Key Performance Indicator (KPI) evaluations with self & manager reviews.
- **SOP Management:** Built-in Standard Operating Procedure library with version controls, review frequencies, and employee digital sign-off.

### 4. 100% Data Privacy & Offline Security
- **Local Database:** Encrypted SQLite/local database file stored securely on your PC drive.
- **Automatic Backups:** Daily backup created every time MpxHR closes, with optional 1-click sync to your private Google Drive.
- **Zero Lock-in Policy:** If a subscription ends, MpxHR enters a permanent **free view-only mode**. You can always view, print, and export all historical payroll records to Excel.
